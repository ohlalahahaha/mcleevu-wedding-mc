# Approved 16:9 artifact — production contract

The approved artifact is a cinematic, premium wedding-editorial homepage. Match its DESIGN, not any invented text.

## Premium definition
Minimal, elegant, simple. Less content, more care. Important sections are larger and better designed. Decorative elements exist only where they materially improve the composition.

## Composition target
1. HERO: full-width dark navy/candlelit wedding scene. Dense white/ivory roses and candles frame both outer edges. Lee is large, integrated into the scene, holding a microphone. Copy sits left/centre. Header is minimal: MC Lee Vu, About, Video, Contact, EN|VI, one gold Check your date CTA. The main hero content sits lower than a normal landing page, leaving atmospheric space above.
2. QUIET FACT STRIP: four refined facts, not KPI cards: English + Vietnamese; Since 2006; Sydney; One wedding per date.
3. ABOUT + SYDNEY: this is a major designed section, not a stack. About Lee on ivory with real portrait and concise biography; adjacent Sydney Harbour panel with Harbour Bridge/Opera House atmosphere. Flowers frame edges. Include a subtle healthcare/community note and link to Acoustic Hearing Care.
4. WHAT LEE HANDLES: three editorial columns/steps, open layout, no boxed SaaS cards.
5. VIDEO + DATE CHECK: dark showreel block paired with a clean ivory booking/date panel. Video poster must show Lee's full face. Booking must feel integrated and trustworthy.
6. CONTACT + FAQ + FOOTER: concise, refined, dark/navy finish.

## Visual language
Deep navy, warm ivory, restrained champagne gold. High-contrast editorial serif + clean sans. Candlelight bokeh, white florals, subtle paper quality, hairline dividers. No glassmorphism, gradients-for-effect, pill UI, generic card grids, fake handwritten slogans everywhere, fake testimonials, fake awards, fake client counts, or AI people.

## Real media only
Use existing real Lee assets in public/media/. Never regenerate Lee. Use wedding decor photos only as atmosphere/masks/crops. Use Sydney Harbour decor asset as the meaningful Sydney panel.

## Copy rules
Every sentence must explain Lee, his service, his character, his real background, or the next booking action. Delete generic emotional filler. Do not claim "trusted by hundreds", "real couples", or quote an "actual couple" unless sourced.

## Booking
Do not throw price at visitors in a homepage sales band. Keep A$1,000/A$500/A$500 inside the booking flow after date availability. Cloudflare Pages Functions already serve the backend routes; if Lee-specific Supabase/Stripe secrets are absent, the UI must fail honestly and offer direct contact.

## Acceptance
Render 1440 desktop and 390 mobile. Score against the artifact: atmosphere 30, composition 25, real-Lee integration 20, clarity/copy/UX 25. Below 80/100 = FAIL and must be revised. Tests/build/CI do not override a visual FAIL.
